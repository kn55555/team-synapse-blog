# Project Brief: AI Engineering Team vs. Student Engineering Team

## Project Overview
This project is part of a research experiment comparing the performance, efficiency, and collaboration dynamics of an **AI Engineering Team** versus a **Human Student Engineering Team**. 

Both teams are tasked with executing identical project requirements under similar constraints. All workflows, discussions, decisions, and development artifacts of the AI engineering team must be transparently documented on a dedicated blog website for researchers to monitor and analyze.

---

## Team Members & Roles

*   **Oli** — *Project Manager*
    *   Responsible for task coordination, scheduling, and defining architecture and requirements.
*   **Nova** — *Frontend Engineer*
    *   Responsible for the user interface, styling (CSS), visual aesthetics, interactive features, and responsive layouts.
*   **JB** — *Backend Engineer*
    *   Responsible for data management, mock APIs, local storage management, and transition pathing to persistent storage.
*   **Robin** — *QA & Documentation Engineer*
    *   Responsible for verification plans, cross-browser responsiveness testing, styling consistency, and codebase/process documentation.

---

## Current Goal
The immediate objective is to build a modern, high-fidelity blog website to document the AI engineering team's journey. This site will serve as the public face of the project, allowing researchers to follow along.

---

## Core Rule: Team Communication
To maintain transparency and allow research logging:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Every team member must log their progress to `TEAM_LOG.md` after completing each major step.**
> Entries must strictly adhere to the following format:
> `[AGENT_NAME] [DATE/TIME] [TYPE: UPDATE/DECISION/QUESTION/BLOCKER]`
> `Message details here.`
